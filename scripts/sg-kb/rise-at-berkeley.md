WHAT IT IS: a small community of 48 furnished studios, one block from UC Berkeley, for one or two occupants (apply with a roommate for a shared studio). Studios about 359 to 371 sq ft. Kala Bagai Way is the two blocks of former Shattuck Ave between Center and University, renamed in 2020.

IN EVERY STUDIO: kitchen with appliances, in-unit washer and dryer, A/C and heat, wood-look flooring, keyless entry, cable-ready. Layouts: Modern (twin XL beds and desks), Trendy (loft twin XL over desks), or a mix.

BUILDING: rooftop deck with BBQ, Luxer One package lockers, secure bike racks, gated access, access-controlled elevator, security cameras, retail at street level, 1 gig AT&T fiber throughout, cellular booster. No parking on site.

LEASE TERMS (AppFolio): 12-month leases; short terms cost 10% over base; ask about Spring. Security deposit $1,000. Private studios: PG&E paid directly, water and garbage billed back. Shared studios: $125/month flat fee covering water, garbage, electric and in-room internet. Units are occupied, so tours need 48 hours' notice. 3D tours: https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=ip2ePQJcguL (single), https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=qX4hzxv45iE (double).

WALKS: Downtown Berkeley BART about 3 minutes, Doe Library 10, Sather Gate 11, Sproul Plaza 12. UC shuttle and AC Transit stops at the door. Leasing team also at (510) 704-1240.
