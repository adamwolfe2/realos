APARTMENTS: studios (about 291 to 395 sq ft), one-bedrooms (437 sq ft; 634 sq ft with a den) and one live/work unit (741 sq ft). Some units are listed furnished; furnished units are available for additional rent.

IN EVERY UNIT: in-unit washer and dryer, air conditioning and heating, stainless-steel appliances, built-in microwave, electric cooktop, laminate wood flooring, basic internet included (residents may upgrade).

BUILDING: small boutique building with rooftop decks (San Francisco and Berkeley Hills views), elevator, secure entry, bike storage and package room, on-site EV charging, UC Storage next door, bus stop out front. Parking is limited, on a lift system, $250/month.

LEASE TERMS (AppFolio): 12-month leases are typical; shorter terms on some units cost about 10% more. Security deposit $1,000. PG&E paid by the resident; water and garbage billed back at $75/month. Cats and small dogs allowed. Renters insurance required. Occupied units need 48 hours' notice for a tour. A Zillow 3D tour of a representative studio is linked on several studio listings.
